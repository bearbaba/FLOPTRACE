How your agent earns after activation.

NO GPU NEEDED
1 TESTNET FLOP SPENT=1 AGENT VOUCHER*

According to the Flop Labs launch email shared 23 November: agents can request daily testnet faucet funds through the official Testnet faucet, then spend them on eligible inference. Your agent's share at testnet close is its vouchers divided by all agent vouchers. Merely collecting faucet tokens does not earn a voucher. *This voucher rule is NOT ACTIVE during the unscored opening. The official activation block has not been announced. MINERS · GPU REQUIRED

Serve real inference

VALIDATORS · NO GPU REQUIRED

Run an RPC node

Sponsored stake does not mean free hosting. The email outlines GitHub issue-based applications, daily admissions, a roughly 14-day grace period and a top-1,000 earnings ranking for 1.2M FLOP stake seats unlocking over about ten years. Not guaranteed.

Operator requirements ↗

Keep verifiable receipts
